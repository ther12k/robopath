# Grown-Up's Guide to Robo Paths

**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared for Guardians and Educators**

## 1. What is Robo Paths?

Robo Paths is a playful, spatial puzzle game designed to introduce computational thinking and early coding concepts to children without intimidation. Through tangible, symbol-based command blocks, players direct friendly robot companions across floating isometric tile worlds.

### Core Learning Goals
- **Sequencing & Direction:** Learning that machines execute instructions sequentially and rotation differs from sideways movement.
- **Problem Solving & Debugging:** Learning through visible feedback when a robot stops at an obstacle or missing turn, without shame, countdown timers, or game-over penalties.
- **Loops & Pattern Recognition (World 2+):** Discovering how repetition condenses repetitive tasks into compact, efficient programs.
- **State & Causality (World 3+):** Stepping on switches to permanently open matching gates.

---

## 2. Privacy & Data Handling

Robo Paths is committed to child-first privacy and safety:
- **Zero Accounts:** No child names, email addresses, birthdays, or passwords are ever requested.
- **Zero Ads or In-App Purchases:** No microtransactions, paid hints, or commercial tracking.
- **100% Local Storage:** All stars, unlocked levels, and program drafts are stored entirely in your device's browser (via IndexedDB and LocalStorage).
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not collect telemetry or track gameplay sessions.

---

## 3. Managing Progress: Export, Import & Erase

In the **Settings & Grown-Up Area** (gear icon on the title screen):
- **Backup & Export:** You can export your child's progress as a clean JSON file to save or transfer to another browser.
- **Restoring / Importing:** You can import a previously exported save file (validated with strict limits).
- **Reset All Progress:** If you want to start fresh, you can erase all local data with an explicit double-confirmation step.

---

## 4. Accessibility and Play Guidance

- **Audio & Reduced Motion:** Both sound effects and motion effects can be independently toggled in the Settings dialog.
- **Accessible Board Explorer:** An alternate semantic board reader is available on every level, providing a linear, keyboard-navigable description of coordinates, items, gates, and robot facing for screen reader users.
- **Target Sizes:** All interactive controls are sized to at least 48×48 CSS pixels for comfortable touch navigation on mobile devices.
