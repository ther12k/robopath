## Outcome and implementation scope

- Recruit approximately 6–8 participants through adults under a reviewed protocol
- Observe first use, deliberate debugging, robot choice and content progression
- Document findings and revise high-friction interactions/puzzles

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Permission/assent and data minimization are documented
- [ ] Actual observations replace assumed usability pass rates
- [ ] Critical confusion/accessibility findings are fixed or block release

## Required validation

- [ ] Pilot the protocol with an adult before child sessions
- [ ] Retest changed teaching levels and record remaining uncertainty
