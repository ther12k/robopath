## Outcome and implementation scope

- Implement add/insert/reorder/replace/delete/clear actions with stable node IDs
- Support bounded undo and a distinct draft-to-runnable validation step
- Keep capacity accounting centralized and reusable by all input methods

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least 50 current-level operations are undoable
- [ ] Canceling an edit leaves draft unchanged
- [ ] Clear and reset have separate effects; capacity is never silently exceeded

## Required validation

- [ ] Property-test reorder/undo and unique IDs
- [ ] Test replacement at full capacity and empty draft serialization
